Special Diet

"Fuel Your Fitness: Nutrition & Diet Plans"
Lentil Salad

Lentils are a great source of plant-based protein. Combine cooked lentils with mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, red onion, and feta cheese. Dress with a lemon vinaigrette for a satisfying and protein-rich salad.

Chickpea and Vegetable Stir-Fry

Chickpeas are a versatile legume that can be used in many dishes. Stir-fry cooked chickpeas with mixed vegetables, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil for a flavorful and protein-rich dinner.

Egg White Omelette with Spinach and Feta Cheese

Egg whites are a complete protein source that is low in fat and high in protein. Make an omelette with egg whites, spinach, and feta cheese for a satisfying meal that is perfect for breakfast or any time of the day.

Salmon with Sweet Potato Mash

Salmon is packed with omega-3 fatty acids and high-quality protein, making it ideal for muscle recovery and overall health. Serve it with a side of mashed sweet potatoes for complex carbohydrates that provide lasting energy.

Dal Tadka

Dal Tadka is often served with rice or Indian bread (roti) and is a nutritious dish high in protein, fiber, and essential nutrients. The spices in Dal Tadka, like turmeric and cumin, have anti-inflammatory properties that can help with post-workout recovery.

Palak Paneer

Palak Paneer nutritious, as it is rich in iron, calcium, and vitamins A and C from the spinach, and provides a good amount of protein from the paneer, making it a wholesome vegetarian meal.
